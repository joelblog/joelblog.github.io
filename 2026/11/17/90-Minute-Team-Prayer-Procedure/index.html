90-Minute Team Prayer Procedure

(For ~10 people)

1. Welcome & Opening (8–10 minutes)

  • Greet everyone warmly as they arrive; invite them to sit in a circle if possible.
  • Brief welcome and purpose: “We’re here to pray together as a team for our church, our people, and the needs God places on our hearts.”
  • Opening prayer (led by host or volunteer): Thank God for the gathering, invite the Holy Spirit, and ask for unity and guidance.
  • Optional short icebreaker (1–2 minutes): One sentence each — “How are you coming into this time?” or “One thing you’re grateful for today.”

2. Scripture Focus (5–7 minutes)

  • Read a short passage aloud (e.g., Philippians 4:6–7, Matthew 18:19–20, James 5:16, or 1 Timothy 2:1–2).
  • One person can briefly share one observation or how the verse speaks to prayer (keep it to 1–2 minutes).
  • Optional: A moment of silence to let the Word settle.

3. Sharing Prayer Requests (15–18 minutes)

  • Invite people to share briefly (aim for 30–60 seconds each).
  • Categories that work well for a team:
    • Personal / family needs
    • Church life, leaders, ministries, or upcoming events
    • Community or broader concerns
  • Have one person jot down the main requests so nothing is forgotten.
  • Host gently keeps the pace so everyone who wants to share can do so.

4. Main Prayer Time (45–50 minutes)

This is the heart of the meeting. Choose one primary format (or combine two):

Option A – Guided Group Prayer (recommended for focus)

  • Host or a volunteer leads through the collected requests one by one or in clusters.
  • After each request (or cluster), pause for open prayer: anyone may pray aloud briefly.
  • Keep a relaxed pace; allow silence.

Option B – Open Circle Prayer

  • Go around the circle (or popcorn style). Each person prays for 1–3 minutes, then passes to the next or invites others.
  • People may pray for specific requests already shared or whatever is on their heart.

Option C – Small Groups + Whole Group

  • Split into 2–3 smaller groups of 3–4 for 20–25 minutes of more personal prayer.
  • Re-gather for the last 15–20 minutes and pray together over the main shared needs or for the church as a whole.

Tips for the prayer time

  • Encourage short, sincere prayers rather than long monologues.
  • It’s fine to pray in turn, out loud together, or with periods of quiet.
  • Host can gently redirect if needed: “Let’s bring this back to prayer…” or “Who would like to pray next?”

5. Closing (7–10 minutes)

  • Final open prayer: anyone may offer a short closing prayer of thanks or commitment.
  • Host closes with a brief prayer summarizing the time, asking God’s blessing on everyone present and on the church.
  • Optional: One sentence each — “One thing I’m taking from this time” or a simple “Amen” / word of encouragement.
  • Announce any next meeting or follow-up if relevant.
  • Thank everyone for coming and dismiss with a warm blessing.

Quick Timeline Summary

  • Welcome & Opening: 0–10 min
  • Scripture: 10–17 min
  • Sharing requests: 17–35 min
  • Main prayer: 35–80/85 min
  • Closing: 80–90 min

Practical Notes

  • Start and end on time so people know what to expect.
  • Have tissues, water, and a notebook/phone ready for requests.
  • Be flexible — if the Spirit is moving in one area, it’s okay to stay longer there and shorten something else.
  • Keep the atmosphere warm, respectful, and unhurried.
